— The idea, in 90 seconds

The token is your loyalty card.

You hand the barista a card. They swipe it. Their screen lights up with your usual: oat milk, extra hot, one pump vanilla, light foam. You never recite the order. They just make it.

Your token works the same way. It is a short string starting with pp_. The Purely Personal connector is the till that remembers your order. Claude is the barista who looks you up and makes the drink. You set it up once, and from that moment every chat already knows your voice, your ICP, your brand and your language.

Without a token
  • Open a chat, paste your Voice DNA
  • Then paste your ICP
  • Then ask for the post
  • Tomorrow, all three again
  • Scheduled tasks cannot work at all, because nobody is there to paste
With a token
  • Open a chat, ask for the post
  • Voice and ICP load themselves
  • Tomorrow, same
  • Scheduled tasks work, because the token goes with them
  • Your post history and results are stored against it
Is it a password?

Treat it like one, but do not panic about it. Nobody can do anything with your token unless they also have the Purely Personal connector installed. Do not paste it in the group chat. If you lose it, ask Claude in the same conversation, or open your Project instructions, where it lives permanently.

— Step 1 · About 3 minutes

Get your token.

One prompt. One token back. You do this once in your life.

1
Copy your Voice DNA and your ICP≈ 30 seconds
Open both documents. Select all, copy. You will paste each one into the prompt below, so having both ready before you start saves you a lot of tab switching.
2
Open a new chat and paste this≈ 90 seconds

Any chat with the Purely Personal connector switched on. Replace every bracket with your real content before you send.

Token prompt · replace the brackets
Use the Purely Personal connector. Call set_my_context with
these values. Do not ask me follow-up questions, just call
the tool and give me my token.

voice_dna: [paste your entire Voice DNA here]

icp: [paste your entire ICP here]

email: [your email]

linkedin_url: [your LinkedIn profile URL]

language_preference: en
If you write in another language

Change language_preference to your language instead of en. The options are en English, es Spanish, fr French, de German, pt Portuguese, vi Vietnamese, id Bahasa Indonesia, zh Chinese. Set it here and every post, every scheduled task and every DM comes out in that language, in your voice, without you asking again.

Look for the tool-call card

You want a card in the chat showing purely-personal · set_my_context, not a paragraph of text. If Claude just replied in prose, send: Call the set_my_context tool with the exact values above. Do not ask questions.

3
Copy the token≈ 10 seconds

Within a couple of seconds Claude replies with something like this. The token is the pp_ string.

What comes back
{
  "token": "pp_a3k9m2x7p4q1",
  "action": "created",
  "saved_fields": ["voice_dna","icp","email","linkedin_url"]
}

Copy it. If you lose it, it is still in this conversation, and after Step 2 it will also be in your Project instructions.

Checkpoint · type TOKEN in the chat

A pp_ string on your clipboard. Nothing else needed yet.

Two questions that always come up

Can I have more than one? Yes. Run set_my_context again with a different business's voice and ICP and you get a second token. Keep each one in its own Claude Project so they never collide.

How do I start over? Ask Claude to call clear_my_context with your token, then run set_my_context again for a clean one.

— Step 3 · About 5 minutes

Move it into Cowork.

Chat is a conversation you drive. Cowork is a place where work happens whether or not you are watching. Same project, same token, different room.

1
Open Cowork in Claude Desktop
The centre button in the desktop app. Inside you will find projects, scheduled tasks, live artifacts and Dispatch.
2
Find or import your project
Click Projects. If AI Marketing Employee is there, open it. If not, click New project and choose import, then search for it by name. Projects made in chat do not always appear in Cowork on their own.
3
Check your connectors work in here

Cowork runs connectors differently from chat, so a connector that works perfectly in chat can be silent in Cowork. Ask for something small and watch for a tool-call card:

Quick check
Use get_my_context with my token and tell me
what you know about my business.
If Apify is silent in Cowork but fine in chat

You installed it from the connector directory rather than as a custom connector. The directory version runs on your own machine and Cowork often cannot start it. Remove it, then add it back as a custom connector using https://mcp.apify.com. Two minutes, and it fixes it permanently.

4
Add the Cowork instructions

Cowork keeps its own instructions, separate from the chat project. Paste this block in and replace the placeholder with your token.

Cowork project instructions · replace the token
My purely-personal token is pp_xxxxxxxxxxxx.

On every Purely Personal tool call, pass
user_token = pp_xxxxxxxxxxxx.

When a scheduled task runs, always:
1. Load my voice and ICP from the token first
2. Write in my voice, never a generic one
3. Never use my banned words, and never use em dashes
4. Save every output to a document and tell me where it is

Never invent client results, numbers or testimonials
I have not given you.

Save. This is the last time you type your token today.

5
Give Cowork one real job, live

Before we schedule anything, watch it work while you do something else.

Run this in Cowork now
Over the next 20 minutes, do all of this in the background:

1. Use analyze_competitor on these profiles:
   - [competitor 1 URL]
   - [competitor 2 URL]

2. Use find_prospects with my ICP to find 10 people.
   keywords: [your keywords from yesterday]
   location: [your location]

3. For the 10 best, draft a personal LinkedIn DM in my voice
   that references something specific from their profile.

4. Write me one LinkedIn post about the biggest gap the
   competitor scan found.

5. Save everything into a single document called
   "Monday Morning Brief".

Ping me here when you are done.

Then switch tabs and go back to the session. It carries on without you, which is the entire idea.

— Step 4 · About 5 minutes

Set one scheduled task. Not five.

There are five good ones below. Pick the one that matches how you actually work, set it today, and add a second next week once the first has fired twice. People who set all five on Saturday have all five switched off by Wednesday.

How to set any of them

In Cowork, inside your project, paste the block and press enter. Claude creates the scheduled task. Adjust the day and time to your timezone before you send.

Task 1Start here if you post regularly

Sunday 19:00 · the weekly content drop

The one that changes your week. Sunday evening it builds everything you need for the next seven days. Monday morning it is waiting.

Paste into Cowork
Create a scheduled task.

Name: Weekly Content Drop
Schedule: every Sunday at 7pm my time

Each run, using my token and my weekly-content-engine skill:
1. Call get_my_post_history so you do not repeat a topic
   from the last 30 days
2. Pick this week's theme from the gaps in my market
3. Write one LinkedIn long-form post for Monday
4. Write a 7-slide carousel brief for Wednesday
5. Write a cheatsheet for Friday
6. Call score_post on all three. Anything under 4 out of 4,
   fix it and score again before showing me
7. Call generate_image for the Monday cover and the
   carousel cover slide
8. Save it all to one document called "Week of [date]"
   and email me the link
Task 2Start here if you struggle to start

Every morning 07:00 · today's post, already decided

The smallest useful task and the easiest to keep. It removes the daily question of what to post, which is what actually stops most people.

Paste into Cowork

Name: Morning Post Brief

Feeding it

This only works if something is logging results. When you publish, tell Claude to call log_published_post. When a DM comes in, log_dm_received. When a call gets booked, log_call_booked. Thirty seconds a day, and by week four you can see which posts actually pay.
